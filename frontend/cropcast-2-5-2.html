The problem

Cameroon's agricultural supply chain is broken.

🚫

Farmers can't reach buyers

Most farmers are limited to selling locally, so they sell at low prices or watch produce go to waste.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Too many middlemen

Multiple intermediaries inflate prices by 30–50%. Buyers overpay while farmers underearn.

📄

No price transparency

Farmers price blindly. There's no central, reliable source of daily market prices.

🚚

Unreliable transport

Getting produce from farm to buyer is costly, slow, and unpredictable.

About Theraprice

Market intelligence for the people who grow Cameroon's food.

Theraprice is a produce marketplace and price-prediction platform, piloting in Yaoundé and Douala. We built it because farmers deserve the same market visibility that traders on a stock exchange take for granted.

🌾

Farmer / Seller

Verified identity, lists produce, posts Moments, and receives sell-or-wait price alerts.

🛒

Buyer

Individual or institutional browses, orders, pays, tracks delivery, and comments on Moments.

🛡️

Administrator

Reviews farmer identity submissions and product listings, and monitors data quality.

Verification & trust, built in from day one

Because Theraprice moves real money and real produce, every farmer account is verified before its listings go live — not just after a complaint. Identity checks and a digital contract keep every account on the platform accountable.
